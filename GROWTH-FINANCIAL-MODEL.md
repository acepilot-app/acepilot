## BREAK-EVEN & PROFITABILITY ANALYSIS

### Unit Economics (Per Paid Customer)

```
Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):
- Average monthly revenue: $29
- Average retention: 36 months (3 years @ 3% churn)
- LTV = $29 × 36 = $1,044

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):
- Blended CAC (organic + paid): $60-80
- CAC payback period: 2-3 months ($60-80 ÷ $29/month)
- LTV:CAC ratio: 1,044 ÷ $70 = 14.9:1 (healthy, >3:1 is breakeven)

**Conclusion**: Profitable on day 1 from paid customers
```

### Company Break-Even

```
Fixed Costs (Monthly):
- Infrastructure (servers, APIs): $100
- SaaS tools (email, analytics): $50
- Payment processor (Stripe): 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- Team (you): $0 (founder sweat equity)
────────────────────────────
Total monthly fixed: ~$150

Variable Costs:
- Per paying customer: ~$5-10 (API costs, payment fees)
- Per acquired customer: ~$20-40 (paid ads)

Break-Even Calculation:
- Need 6-8 paying customers @ $29/month = $174-232/month
- This covers fixed + variable costs
- **Break-even**: Month 1-2 (with Show HN spike + early conversions)

Cash Flow Break-Even:
- More relevant: when monthly revenue > cumulative costs
- Happens: Month 3-4 (when MRR exceeds monthly cash burn)
```

## PAID AD SPEND EFFICIENCY (ROI by Channel)

### Google Ads (Search)

```
Campaign Metrics:
- Avg CPC (cost per click): $2-4
- CTR (click-through rate): 2-5%
- Landing page conversion: 10-15% (click → signup)
- Free trial conversion to paid: 5-8%

Example: $500/month budget
- Budget: $500
- Clicks: 500/2.5 = 125 clicks (using $4 CPC)
- Signups: 125 × 12% = 15 free signups
- Paid conversions: 15 × 6% = 1 paid customer
- CAC: $500 ÷ 1 = $500 (ouch!)

Better scenario (optimized):
- CPC: $2.50
- CTR: 4%
- Landing page conversion: 20%
- Free → paid: 8%

With $500:
- Clicks: 200
- Signups: 40
- Paid: 3 customers
- CAC: $167 (still high, but LTV:CAC = 6:1 is OK)

**Recommendation**: Start with $50-100/week to test, optimize, then scale
```

### Newsletter Sponsorships

```
Metrics:
- CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions): $50-100
- Open rate: 30-40% (dev tool newsletters)
- CTR (click rate): 5-10%
- Landing page conversion: 15-20%
- Free → paid: 5-8%

Example: $200 sponsorship (Pointer.io or similar)
- Impressions: 200 / $0.075 (mid-range CPM) = 2,667 impressions
- Opens: 2,667 × 35% = 933 opens
- Clicks: 933 × 7% = 65 clicks
- Signups: 65 × 17% = 11 free signups
- Paid: 11 × 6% = 0.7 ≈ 1 paid customer
- CAC: $200 (but much higher quality user than Google Ads)

Newsletter sponsorship reality:
- High quality leads (developers who actively read newsletters)
- Lower volume, but better intent
- Better for brand building
- Viral potential (newsletter features you positively)

**Recommendation**: Best ROI for dev tools. Do 2-3 sponsorships/month
```

### Twitter Ads

```
Metrics:
- CPE (cost per engagement): $0.50-2.00
- CTR: 0.5-2%
- Typical CAC: $200-500+

Why Twitter ads underperform for dev tools:
- Developers ignore ads
- High CPE but low conversion
- Better for consumer products

**Recommendation**: Skip Twitter ads. Use organic Twitter instead (free)
```
