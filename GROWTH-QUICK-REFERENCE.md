## RED FLAGS (Early Warning System)

### 🚩 Product

- [ ] Day 7 retention < 20% (product doesn't solve real problem)
- [ ] Time to first value > 10 minutes (too much friction)
- [ ] Churn > 15% monthly (customers unhappy)

### 🚩 Growth

- [ ] MoM growth < 5% for 2+ months (stalled)
- [ ] Free → paid conversion < 1% (pricing wrong or audience wrong)
- [ ] CAC > LTV (not sustainable)

### 🚩 Community

- [ ] No Discord engagement after 2 weeks (nobody cares)
- [ ] Zero blog post comments (content not resonating)
- [ ] Show HN gets <10 upvotes (poor product-market fit signals)
