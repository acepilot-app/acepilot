## Key Numbers to Remember

### Growth Targets

- **Month 1**: 200 free users, 5-10 paid ($116-290 MRR)
- **Month 3**: 700 free users, 25+ paid ($725+ MRR) — must hit 20%+ MoM growth
- **Month 6**: 2,500+ free users, 100+ paid ($3K MRR)
- **Month 12**: 8,000+ free users, 600+ paid ($3K-5K MRR)

### Unit Economics

- **LTV**: $1,000+ per paid customer (29/mo × 36 months)
- **CAC**: $50-100 (blended organic + paid)
- **LTV:CAC**: 10+:1 (excellent)
- **Payback**: 2-4 months

### Benchmarks by Channel

- **Organic (SEO)**: $0 CAC, 6-month lag, compounds forever
- **Newsletter sponsorship**: $50-100 CAC, 2-3 month ROI (best for dev tools)
- **Google Ads**: $100-200 CAC, 3-4 month ROI, expensive
- **Referrals**: $15-20 CAC, 1-month ROI (if incentivized)
- **Show HN**: $0 CAC, instant (one-time spike)

### Success Indicators

- Free → paid conversion: 5%+ (month 3+)
- Monthly churn: <5% (healthy)
- DAU/MAU: 50%+ (engagement)
- Viral coefficient: >1.0 (self-sustaining growth)

**Q: Which document should I read first?**
A: GROWTH-EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md (8 min). Then drill into specific areas based on what you need to do this week.

**Q: I'm ready to spend $500/month. Where do I start?**
A: Read FINANCIAL-MODEL section "Paid Channel Allocation" + RESEARCH section "$500/Month Budget" + QUICK-REFERENCE "Content Calendar". Start with $100 test budget, not $500.

**Q: How should I track progress?**
A: Use the metrics in QUICK-REFERENCE "Dashboard KPIs" (weekly) + FINANCIAL-MODEL "Month-by-Month P&L" (monthly). Copy the template into a spreadsheet or Notion.

**Q: Should I hire someone now?**
A: No. Bootstrap for months 1-3, prove product-market fit, then hire. By month 2-3, hire a freelancer for blog writing/editing ($100-200/month). By month 4-6, if you hit $1K+ MRR, consider hiring your first engineer or marketer.

**Q: What if my metrics don't match the projections?**
A: That's normal. The projections are "if everything goes well." Use the RED FLAG THRESHOLDS in QUICK-REFERENCE to diagnose what's broken:

- CAC too high? (focus on organic)
- Conversion too low? (improve product or messaging)
- Churn too high? (product doesn't solve the problem)

**Q: Should I raise money?**
A: Bootstrap first. Prove metrics through month 3. If you hit $500+ MRR + 20% MoM growth, you're fundable. If you miss these, raise money to fix the problem (not just to scale).
