When to choose AcePilot

  • You have an existing project — AcePilot works with your codebase, not a blank canvas. It reads your git history, patterns, and decisions.

  • You want autonomous multi-task execution — god mode: one command ships entire features with review and deployment.

When to choose Bolt.new

  • You need a quick prototype — Bolt.new generates working apps from a prompt in under a minute.

  • You're starting from scratch — Bolt excels at greenfield generation, less at iterating on existing code.
