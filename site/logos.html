The Winner: V04 — Wide Bold + Diamond Counter

Why V04 wins

Concept: The diamond counter is the only design that has built-in meaning at two levels. At 16px it's a bold readable A. At 32px+ the ♦ (Ace of Diamonds) is visible — "the highest card." No other variation earns its counter shape.

Proportions: The 52×42 canvas (wider than tall) gives an A that feels grounded and powerful rather than spindly. The 11px leg width is the thickest of all designs, making it bulletproof at small sizes. The 8px crossbar (28-20=8 units thick) is the first design that has a truly visible crossbar at 16×16 favicon size.

Diamond detail: The diamond's bottom vertex lands exactly at the crossbar level — a mathematical coincidence that looks intentional. It creates a crossbar that "holds" the diamond like a display pedestal. You can't unsee it.
